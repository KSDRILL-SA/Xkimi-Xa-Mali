> **Why this exists.** PAIA requires **every private body** — including a small
> voluntary association — to compile a manual describing what records it holds and
> how a person can request access to them. Exemptions for small bodies have been
> granted and allowed to lapse at various times, and the safe position is to have
> one. It must be made available on the body's website if it has one, and to the
> Information Regulator on request.
>
> This is short by design. A PAIA manual is a signpost, not a disclosure.

## 3. Records held by the Foundation

The Foundation holds the following categories of records. Listing a category here
does **not** mean the records in it are automatically available — access is
decided on request, under the grounds for refusal in Chapter 4 of PAIA.

### 3.1 Governance

- Constitution of the Foundation
- Minutes and resolutions of general meetings
- Register of office bearers
- Records of registration with regulatory and self-regulatory bodies

### 3.2 Membership

- Register of members
- Applications and invitations to join
- Records of member status, suspension, resignation and expulsion
- Consents given by members

### 3.3 Financial

- Records of contributions by each member
- Records of payments made from the pool, and the Goal each served

- Bank statements and records of the Foundation's bank account
- Debit order mandates
- Annual financial statements

### 3.4 Operational

- Audit log of administrative actions
- Records of communications sent to members
- System access and login records

### 3.5 Statutory

- Records kept under the Protection of Personal Information Act
- Records kept under the Income Tax Act, if applicable

## 4. Records available without a formal request

The following are provided to the person entitled to them without needing a PAIA
request:

| Record | Available to | How |
|---|---|---|
| A member's own contribution history | That member | In the member portal, at any time, downloadable |
| A member's own personal information | That member | In the member portal |
| The status and progress of every Goal | All members | In the member portal |
| The constitution | All members | On request to leadership |
| Annual financial statements | All members | At the annual general meeting |

A member does not need to invoke PAIA to see their own record. This is deliberate:
the Foundation's position is that a member's record belongs to that member.

## 5. How to request access

### 5.1 Form

A request must be made on the **prescribed form** published by the Information
Regulator, and delivered to the Information Officer at the address in clause 1.

### 5.2 What the request must contain

- Sufficient detail to identify the record requested.
- Sufficient detail to identify the requester, and an address for reply.
- The form of access required.
- If the request is made on behalf of another person, proof of authority.
- **If the request is to exercise or protect a right**, a statement of what that
  right is and how the record would assist in exercising or protecting it.

### 5.3 Fees

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Request fee | As prescribed by regulation |
| Access fee | As prescribed, based on reproduction and search time |

A requester seeking their **own** personal information does not pay a request fee.

### 5.4 Decision

The Information Officer will decide within **30 days** of receiving the request
and notify the requester in writing. Where the period is extended, the requester
will be told before the 30 days expire, with reasons.

---

## 6. Grounds for refusal

Access may or must be refused where the record contains, among other grounds set
out in Chapter 4 of PAIA:

- the **personal information of a third party**, the disclosure of which would be
  unreasonable — in this Foundation's case, this is the most likely ground, since
  most records concern identifiable members;
- **commercial information** of a third party;
- information supplied **in confidence**, where disclosure could prejudice the
  supplier;
- information whose disclosure could endanger a person's **life or physical
  safety**;
- information protected by **legal professional privilege**; or
- records of **research** information.

Where a request is refused, the requester will be given reasons and told of their
right to apply to a court, or to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

---

## 7. Remedies

A requester who is dissatisfied may:

1. lodge a **complaint with the Information Regulator**; or
2. apply to a **court** with jurisdiction.

There is **no internal appeal** against a decision of a private body under PAIA.
