## Why this amendment is needed

Clause 6.1 as signed names a single account:

> Capitec Business, account holder KSDRILL SA (Pty) Ltd, account number
> 1055722831, branch code 450105 — held in custody for the Foundation's members
> under clause 1.4A.

That was correct on the assumption in force when it was signed: that the
Foundation's collections application would be approved, and that the Capitec
account would be the settlement account into which a collections partner paid.

The application was declined. There are no collections and therefore nothing to
settle, and contributions are instead paid directly by members — by transfer or
in cash — into the Foundation's **ABSA** account and recorded by an
administrator against the member and the month, with proof of payment.

The result is that the signed constitution names an account that is not the
account contributions are actually paid into. That is not a drafting nicety: it
is the clause a bank, NASASA, or a member checks first, and it should say what is
true.

The amendment does two things:

1. **Names the account contributions are actually paid into today.**
2. **Provides for the second account** without needing a further amendment when a
   collections partner is appointed — because the applicant will be KSDRILL SA
   (Pty) Ltd, and a partner settles into the applicant's own account.

It does **not** change who the money belongs to, what it may be spent on, or who
may move it. Those are clauses 6.2 and 6.4, and they are untouched.

## Clause 14.2 has been checked

An amendment may not do any of four things. This one does none of them:

| 14.2 bars | This amendment |
|---|---|
| (a) removing the requirement that leadership action be recorded | Untouched. Every recorded payment still carries a name and a time |
| (b) permitting money to leave the pool other than for an agreed Goal | Untouched. Clause 6.2 is not amended |
| (c) granting leadership an exemption from a member obligation | Untouched. Leaders pay the same amount on the same terms |
| (d) raising the membership cap above fifty | Untouched |

## RESOLVED

**1.** That clause 6.1 of the Constitution be deleted and replaced with the
following:

> **6.1** All contributions are held in dedicated bank accounts of the
> Foundation, used **only** for the Foundation's funds and never for the income
> or expenses of any other person or business, and never in the personal account
> of any member or leader.
>
> **6.1.1 The contribution account.** Contributions paid by members are received
> into the Foundation's ABSA account, details of which are recorded in the
> register of banking particulars maintained by the Treasurer and disclosed to
> every member inside the Foundation's system.
>
> **6.1.2 The settlement account.** Where the Foundation appoints a collections
> partner, collections are settled into the Capitec Business account of KSDRILL
> SA (Pty) Ltd, account number 1055722831, branch code 450105, held **in custody
> for the Foundation's members** under clause 1.4A. That account is used only for
> the Foundation's funds: never for KSDRILL SA's own income or expenses, and
> never for any other system KSDRILL SA operates.
>
> **6.1.3** Every account named in this clause is subject to clauses 6.2 and 6.4
> without exception, and to the reconciliation requirement in clause 6.7.
>
> **6.1.4** A change to the banking particulars of either account is a decision
> of leadership recorded in writing, disclosed to every member, and reflected in
> the Foundation's system before it takes effect. It is not an amendment of this
> constitution.

**2.** That a new clause 6.7 be inserted:

> **6.7** The balance of every account named in clause 6.1 is reconciled against
> the Foundation's own record of contributions received. A difference between the
> two is reported to leadership and resolved before the month is closed.

**3.** That the Treasurer maintain the register of banking particulars referred
to in clause 6.1.1, and that the particulars in force on the date of this
resolution be recorded in it.

**4.** That this amendment take effect on signature by the office bearers in
accordance with clause 14.3.

Foundation** at ABSA. That must be the name the account is actually held in.
